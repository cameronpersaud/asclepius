The rig

Two masters, one tray, one laptop

The UNO Q reads ten encoders and runs force feedback at 400 Hz. The laptop runs the Python bridge and the Godot sim, and sends contact events back for the grips and arms to push against.

  • 17 kinds of equipment, numbered on the model
  • Motor power: 12 V → e-stop → 2 × 8 V bucks
  • Logic power: laptop USB
